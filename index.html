How to play
  • Everyone has 6 cards face down in two rows. Flip any 2 to start.
  • On your turn, take the top discard or draw from the deck. Swap it for any of your cards, face up or down. If you don't want a card from the deck, discard it. You may then turn over one of your face-down cards, or just end your turn.
  • Points: Joker = −5, A = 1, 2 = 2, 3 to 10 at face value, J and Q = 10, K = 0. The deck has four jokers.
  • Two cards of the same rank in one column score 0 together. Jokers don't cancel, so a pair of jokers still scores −10.
  • Four of a kind: two columns that are both pairs of the same rank score −20 together.
  • When someone turns up their last card, everyone else gets one more turn, then all cards are revealed and scored.
  • Hole scores add up. When anyone reaches the point limit, the game ends and the lowest total wins.
  • Whoever wins a hole goes first on the next one.
